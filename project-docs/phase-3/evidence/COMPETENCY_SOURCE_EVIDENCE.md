## 2. Key Findings & Cross-App Observations
1. **Operational Sets (Apps 283, 716):** Contain Competencies 1 through 6 (`dropdown_choice1_1`..`6_1` / `rating_1_1`..`6_1`). Competencies 7 and 8 are `ABSENT` by design.
2. **Management Sets (Apps 305, 307, 310, 643):** Contain all 8 Competencies with active Appraiser 1 and Appraiser 2 fields. Competency 6 (`COMP_COCE`) is evaluated but strictly excluded from `sum_rating`, resulting in $N_{\text{included}} = 7$ scored items ($\times 2 \text{ Appraisers} = 14 \text{ ratings}$).
3. **Executive Sets (Apps 640, 715):** Contain all 8 Competencies for Appraiser 1. Second appraiser fields are `ABSENT` (`CURRENT_DEPLOYED_SCORING_APPRAISERS = 1`). Formula normalizes the 7 scored items via `sum_rating = (ratings)*2` divided by 14.
4. **Label Schema Inconsistency (`LABEL_SCHEMA_INCONSISTENCY`):** In Apps 305, 310, and 643, `dropdown_choice7_1` displays the label `"7.Compliance / COCE (1)"` due to a historical copy-paste artifact on Kintone, but its criteria strictly define Leadership (`COMP_LEAD`). In Apps 307, 640, 715, it is correctly labeled as Leadership.
